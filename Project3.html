This is the final product with adjusted coding. The hue of the color shifts when the box is shaken/moved changing its axis. It also changes color in a smooth transition for an added light show. The potentiometer is used to change how fast the automatic color shift is. It is hard to see suble hue shifts with small movements because the color could shift due to the x, y, and z axis. It needs hard abrupt movements to observe its color shift but when running and spinning around with this device it'll be able to capture vibrant colors.



Artist Statement

This project is about being able to make a performance with neopixels. With the use of neopixels used for their vibrant and ability to change many colors as a baseline. There are special inputs to change and interact with the neopixels to show a vibrant display of lights.
